How I work

Four stages

01 · Discover

Understand

I learn about users through research, interviews, and observation to understand their needs, goals, and constraints.

02 · Define

Explore

I analyze research findings to define the core problem, prioritize user needs, and create a clear design plan.

03 · Design

Frame

I sketch ideas, create wireframes, interactive prototypes, and test them with users to gather feedback and improve the design.

04 · Deliver

Deploy

I provide developers with clear design specs and assets, then measure success through user feedback and performance metrics.
