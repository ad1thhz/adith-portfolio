Onetrip Parallax-website

Role
UI/UX Designer
Timeline
2 weeks
Team
Individual
Tools
Figma, Reve, NotebookLm, Photoshop

Overview

ONETRIP is a parallax travel website designed to help users explore Himalayan travel blogs, discover trekking destinations, and follow a guided 5-day trekking plan. The immersive scrolling effects and clear navigation create a more engaging browsing experience while making travel information easier to explore.

My role was to design the UI/UX, creating an immersive parallax scrolling experience with engaging visuals, clear navigation, and accessible travel guidance to make trip planning more enjoyable.

The problem

Travel websites can overwhelm users with scattered information, complex navigation, and unclear trip-planning details. Based on a review of similar websites, ONETRIP was designed to offer a more engaging experience by combining Himalayan travel blogs, trekking information, and a 5-day itinerary in one accessible platform.

Research

Methods: Competitor analysis and travel website review.
Participants: Target users such as adventure seekers and trekking enthusiasts were considered.
Key Insights: Users need clear trekking information, easy access to travel blogs, and a structured itinerary. These insights guided ONETRIP’s design to make Himalayan trip planning simpler and more engaging.

Design solution

Immersive Parallax Scrolling: Created a visually engaging Himalayan experience to inspire travelers.
Clear Navigation: Kept menus simple so users can easily explore blogs, trekking details, and travel guides.
Guided 5-Day Itinerary: Organized trekking information into a structured plan to simplify trip planning.
Strong Call to Action: Added a prominent “Explore More” button to encourage users to discover destinations and guides.

B&B Dashboard

Role
UI/UX Designer
Timeline
2 weeks
Team
Individual
Tools
Figma, Reve, NotebookLm, Photoshop

Overview

Designed a dashboard for Byte & Bean, a tech café, to manage orders, sales, expenses, workspace bookings, and customer activity in one place. The dashboard simplifies daily operations by bringing key business insights and management tools into a single, intuitive interface.

My role was to design the UI/UX, focusing on a clean layout, intuitive navigation, and clear data visualization for efficient café management.

The problem

The café needed a more organized way to manage in-store and online orders, track sales and expenses, and monitor workspace bookings. Scattered information and the need to switch between tasks could make daily management time-consuming. These challenges were identified through an analysis of the café’s operational needs and the dashboard requirements.

Research

Methods: Workflow analysis and competitor research.
Participants: Café owners and staff were considered as primary users.
Key Insights: Users need quick access to order details, real-time sales and expense summaries, and workspace bookings in one dashboard. Clear navigation and easy-to-read data visualizations can help simplify daily operations.

Design solution

The dashboard was designed with a clear visual hierarchy, a warm café-inspired color palette, and a centralized layout to make daily management easier. Key decisions included prioritizing order tracking, highlighting sales and expenses through visual charts, and providing quick access to workspace bookings and expense entry to reduce navigation effort and improve efficiency.
Key Screens: Dashboard, Reports, Orders, and Expense Management.
User Flow: View dashboard → Check orders and sales → Manage expenses or bookings.
